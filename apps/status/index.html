What these checks tell you

Opening this page checks the public website, sign-in page and API database readiness through a separate Cloudflare service. Results refresh every minute and may be cached for 60 seconds. If that service is unavailable, we use the latest published snapshot. Results older than 20 minutes are marked as unavailable.

A passing API check does not confirm email delivery or inbox placement. Sending incidents are also monitored through our provider and queue alerts. If your message is delayed, check its status in the email log before retrying.
